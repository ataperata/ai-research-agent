Permanent Daylight Time? The Clock Debate Returns

By Jordan Reyes

2024-03-08

Several U.S. states have passed laws to adopt permanent daylight saving time if Congress allows it. Federal law lets states stay on standard time all year, as Hawaii and most of Arizona do, but it does not allow permanent daylight saving time.

The U.S. Senate passed the Sunshine Protection Act by unanimous consent in March 2022, but the House of Representatives did not vote on it.

The American Academy of Sleep Medicine supports permanent standard time, arguing that it better matches the body's internal clock.

Supporters of permanent daylight time point to more evening daylight for shopping and recreation, while energy savings now play a smaller role in the debate.
