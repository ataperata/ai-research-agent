Cold Climate Air-Source Heat Pump Specification

By Regional Efficiency Partnership

2024-01-10

The cold climate specification sets minimum performance levels for air-source heat pumps sold in northern regions. To qualify under the specification, a cold-climate heat pump must have a coefficient of performance of at least 1.75 at 5°F (-15°C) when running at maximum capacity.

Listed products must report their heating capacity at 5°F, so installers can check how much heat a system will deliver on a cold day instead of relying on ratings taken at 47°F (8°C).

Qualifying systems must use variable-capacity compressors, which let them run efficiently at part load in mild weather and ramp up during cold snaps.

Program staff recommend sizing a cold-climate heat pump to meet most or all of a home's heating load at the local design temperature, and keeping backup heat only for extreme conditions.
