The Future of Heat Pumps: Lessons from Northern Europe

By Energy Outlook Secretariat

2023-03-01

Heat pumps are common in some of Europe's coldest countries. In Norway, about 60 percent of buildings are equipped with a heat pump, and Sweden and Finland also have high adoption rates.

Heat pump sales in Europe grew by nearly 40 percent in 2022, driven by high natural gas prices and government incentives.

The Nordic experience shows that heat pumps can heat homes through long, cold winters when buildings are well insulated and systems are correctly sized and installed.

Running costs depend heavily on the ratio between electricity and gas prices. Where electricity is expensive relative to gas, savings for households are smaller.
